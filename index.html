THE WORKFLOW

From evidence to an assessment.

A focused three-step workflow designed for a college project demonstration.

01

Image Review

A browser-based vision model looks for visible scratches, dents, cracks, broken lamps, shattered glass and flat tyres.

02

AI Damage Detection

The vision model returns candidate damage regions and confidence scores. Predictions can be wrong.

03

Assessment Report

Review the marked-up image, detected categories, estimated visible-region size and indicative repair-cost ranges.

ACADEMIC PROJECT

Built to demonstrate applied AI.

Vehicle Damage & Insurance Analyzer demonstrates the integration of digital image processing, machine learning, computer vision and natural language processing. It provides a preliminary assessment and should not replace a professional inspection, mechanic's evaluation or an insurer's official assessment.
